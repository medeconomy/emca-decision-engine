EMCA Decision Engine

The ranker takes a patient profile and orders regimens by overall-survival evidence first, then by the adverse-effect profile against the patient's own risks and prior-therapy ledger. Endometrioid and serous carcinoma rank on the trial-wide result and the prespecified dMMR / pMMR strata of the all-histology trials; clear cell ranks on clear-cell-level evidence only, and carcinosarcoma on its own randomised trials. Radiotherapy is ranked alongside systemic therapy in the adjuvant setting. Every number traces to a vault extract, a PubMed record or a DailyMed label.

On the endometrioid and serous pages the badge is the trial-wide reading of each row (OS benefit = OS HR with a CI excluding 1 as printed); on the clear cell and carcinosarcoma pages it is the histotype-level reading: a trial-wide HR from an all-histology trial is shown but labelled "included, not broken out", never as a histotype result. No randomised data exist for neoadjuvant chemotherapy in any of the four histotypes, so the ranker has no neoadjuvant setting. Source links go to PubMed; the 📝 extracts and 📄 PDFs live in the vault.
